# Nutrition review

Source menus are preserved; suggestions are separate editorial guidance. No personal energy target or medical treatment claim is made.

Every whole-meal estimate is marked **Insufficient portion information**. Across this collection, unquantified vegetables, fruit, cooking oil, alternative ingredients, recipe yield or raw/cooked weight prevent a defensible complete calorie/macronutrient total. Specified component weights are retained rather than converted into invented complete portions.

Optional considerations cover food variety, an unspecified whole-grain/pulse component, replacing some saturated cooking fats with unsaturated oils, and less salty protein choices. These are considerations for the overall eating pattern, not a requirement that every meal contain every food group. Rice does not establish that a grain is whole grain.

The general principles were checked against [WHO healthy diet guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet). Ingredient-based allergen flags and packaged-food label cautions are informed by [FDA food allergy information](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-allergies-what-you-need-know). Mustard and molluscs are also flagged when explicitly named; the labels are not a jurisdiction-specific legal allergen declaration. Coconut milk is not treated as dairy. Ambiguous “milk” wording is flagged for checking.

Allergen flags include source alternatives. Unknown sausage, sauce, broth or batter formulations and cross-contact cannot be resolved from the chat. No menu is advertised as allergen-free.
